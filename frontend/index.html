About

What Testimonia does — and doesn't do

This is an evidence-retrieval tool, not a medical advisor. Every answer is generated only from indexed clinical trial records and published abstracts, with citations you can inspect and expand. It does not diagnose, prescribe, or recommend treatment for any individual — for personal health concerns, talk to a clinician.

When the retrieved evidence doesn't support a confident answer, Testimonia says so directly rather than guessing — a deliberate design choice, not a limitation of the interface.

Help

Frequently asked questions

What can I ask?

Questions about trial design, eligibility criteria, outcomes, dosing reported in studies, or findings from published literature — within the indexed conditions (hypertension, type 2 diabetes, breast cancer).

Why did it refuse to answer?

Testimonia only answers when the retrieved evidence actually supports a claim. If your question is outside the indexed data, or asks for a personal diagnosis or dosage, it will say so rather than guess.

What does "low retrieval confidence" mean?

It means the closest matching evidence found wasn't a strong match for your question — the answer may still be useful, but is worth double-checking against the cited sources directly.

How do citations work?

Click any citation under an answer to expand it — you'll see the exact quoted passage the claim was based on, plus source metadata like trial phase or publication year.
